MUSIC BRINGS US TOGETHER

Club Events

Make music, meet musicians, and create unforgettable memories.

What's Happening at SoundStart?

Our events give members opportunities to practice, connect, and share their music.

More Than Just Practice

At SoundStart, music is about bringing people together. Our club activities help members develop their skills while having fun.

Whether you enjoy performing, learning new songs, or simply playing guitar with friends, there is something for everyone.

EXPERIENCE THE MUSIC

Our Club Activities

🎸 Jam Sessions

Members get together to play guitar, practice songs, experiment with music, and learn from each other.

Beginners can participate and build confidence in a relaxed setting.

🎵 Guitar Workshops

Explore guitar techniques, learn new chords, and improve your playing skills.

Workshops help members discover new ways to make music.

🎤 Open Mic Nights

Members have opportunities to share songs, perform for others, and celebrate their musical progress.

You can perform alone or with other club members.

⭐ Group Performances

Work together to prepare songs and perform as a group.

These events celebrate teamwork, creativity, and confidence.

Sample Monthly Event Schedule

Here is an example of what a month at SoundStart could look like.

Week Event Activity
Week 1 Beginner Jam Session Practice basic chords and simple songs.
Week 2 Guitar Workshop Learn new techniques and strumming patterns.
Week 3 Open Mic Night Share music and perform favorite songs.
Week 4 Group Performance Play songs together and celebrate progress.

Note: This is a sample schedule for the SoundStart student project, not a confirmed event calendar.
